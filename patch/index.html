Cutting layout

Each fabric is cut separately, so every fabric gets its own sheets. Red #ff0000 at 0.1 pt, millimetre units.

Size

A quarter inch is the quilting standard. Offsetting the finished shape by it reproduces the usual rules — a half-square triangle comes out at finished + ⅞″, a quarter-square at + 1¼″ — and a laser cuts them exactly, so there is nothing to trim afterwards.

Fabric & cutting

Grain lock only rotates pieces by 180°, so nothing lands on the bias and stretches. Turning it off packs tighter but can pull a block out of square.
