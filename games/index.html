Tanks 1–4 players
After Tiny Tanks, the old browser game.

Top-down tank duels where the last tank standing takes the round. Crates break, power-ups drop, and your shells only bounce off walls if you have picked up a ricochet. Play bots on your own, or make a room with a name and password and send it to friends.

10 maps and a fresh maze · 12 power-ups · free-for-all or red v blue

Derby 1–6 players
A demolition derby over a quarry, in 3D.

Last car running takes the round. Panels dent where you hit them, doors and bumpers hang loose and then come off, and a car that loses a wheel drags. The engine sits in the nose, so the old hands ram in reverse. Fight in a quarry where the edge crumbles, containers drop from the sky and four crushers slam down on anyone parked underneath, or out on a frozen lake where the ice gives way under the cars that drive over it.

2 arenas · 4 cars from compact to school bus · crushers, containers, cracking ice · kill-cam

Barrage 1–4 players
Turn-based artillery, and ground that does not last.

Three guns each, taking turns across a hillside. Set the angle, hold to charge, and let go. Every shell takes a bite out of the land, so the cover you hid behind this turn may not be there the next. Dig someone out with a burrowing shell, leave a mine where they will walk, or save the big one for when it counts.

8 weapons · crates by parachute · squads of three · bots or friends
Play Barrage
