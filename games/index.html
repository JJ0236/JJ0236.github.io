Derby 1–6 players
A demolition derby over a quarry, in 3D.

Last car running takes the round. Panels dent where you hit them, doors and bumpers hang loose and then come off, and a car that loses a wheel drags. The engine sits in the nose, so the old hands ram in reverse. The platform crumbles from the edges, containers drop from the sky and four crushers slam down on anyone parked underneath.

4 cars from compact to school bus · crushers, containers, crumbling edges · kill-cam
