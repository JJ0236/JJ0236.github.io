Last car running takes the round. You are out when your engine dies or you go over the edge.

The car doing the ramming takes less than the car it hits. But the engine sits in the nose, so a head-on hurts you both: old derby hands ram in reverse. A hit in the side tears doors off. Parts hang loose, then come off, and a car that loses a wheel drags.

From 45 seconds the edge crumbles, a ring at a time. Containers drop where the red ring shows. Stay out from under the four crushers when their pads flash.

Pickups: repair (engine +40), armour (half damage), plough (your nose hits twice as hard) and boost.
